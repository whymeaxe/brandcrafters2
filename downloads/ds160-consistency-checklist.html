DS-160 Consistency Checklist

Use this checklist to identify information that should be verified before a DS-160 is submitted. It is an educational resource, not a substitute for legal advice or case-specific review.

Explore Professional Review
Important

Never change a fact merely to make an application appear stronger. Correct errors truthfully and keep records of the information submitted.

Identity and passport

  • Name, date of birth and passport details match the passport exactly.
  • Other names, nationalities and identification details are answered completely.
  • Passport issue and expiry dates are verified.

Travel purpose and itinerary

  • Visa category matches the actual purpose.
  • Planned dates, duration and U.S. locations are internally consistent.
  • Person or organisation paying is correctly identified.

Employment, business and education

  • Current and previous roles, dates and duties are accurate.
  • Business ownership and income information align with supporting records.
  • Education history and gaps are complete.

Family, relatives and U.S. contacts

  • Immediate relatives and relatives in the United States are disclosed accurately.
  • U.S. contact details match the stated purpose and itinerary.
  • Petition or immigration-history questions are answered carefully.

Travel, refusals and prior applications

  • Previous U.S. refusals are disclosed correctly.
  • International travel history is checked against passports.
  • Material differences from earlier forms are understood and explainable.

Before submission

  • Review the complete form, not only the confirmation page.
  • Save a full copy for future reference.
  • Do not submit information you do not understand or cannot explain truthfully.
  • Seek qualified legal advice for criminal, removal, misrepresentation, unlawful-presence or other legal-complexity issues.
