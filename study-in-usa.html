How we shortlist

Fit comes before a long list of university names.

  • Programme curriculum and specialisation
  • Academic entry requirements
  • Career relevance and skill development
  • Total tuition and living cost
  • Funding feasibility
  • Location and student preference
  • Application competitiveness
  • Visa-story coherence
Any institutional commission, representation or commercial relationship should be disclosed to the student. Final selection should remain an informed student decision.
Student profile review

Bring these details to the first session.

  • Academic transcripts and backlogs
  • English-language or standardised test scores
  • Résumé and work experience
  • Preferred subject and career direction
  • Budget and funding sources
  • Previous visa or travel history
  • Target intake and timeline
  • Family priorities and constraints
