# Embassy of Education visual update

## Logo
The original Embassy of Education logo from the supplied source project has been restored site-wide. The temporary Brand Crafters wordmark update has been removed.

## Reference-inspired palette
The visual system is based on the supplied Meridian-style reference screenshot:
- Deep purple: `#3F1C54`
- Deep purple: `#2B123B`
- Accent yellow: `#FFC800`
- Soft yellow: `#FFF6CF`
- Soft lilac: `#F5F0F7`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

The site now uses a white editorial base, purple typography/navigation, yellow CTAs and accents, soft lilac/yellow alternating sections, and deep-purple editorial bands.
