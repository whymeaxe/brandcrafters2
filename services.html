Service boundaries

Clear scope protects both the client and the quality of advice.

What our advisory services can do

  • Review information supplied by the applicant
  • Identify visible contradictions and credibility risks
  • Explain official processes using public government sources
  • Support truthful form preparation and interview readiness
  • Recommend legal referral where necessary

What our advisory services cannot do

  • Guarantee approval or influence a consular officer
  • Access consular notes, internal databases or hidden refusal reasons
  • Create false documents, facts, employment or travel history
  • Act as a law firm unless a separately identified authorised legal professional is engaged
  • Replace official government instructions
