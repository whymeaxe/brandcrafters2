Case-study template

Every published success story should answer seven questions.

  1. Who was the applicant, in anonymised terms?
  2. What was the visa category and previous history?
  3. What visible risks or contradictions were identified?
  4. What service did Embassy of Education provide?
  5. What genuinely changed before the new application?
  6. What was the verified outcome?
  7. What limitation or lesson should future applicants understand?
Never publish:
passport numbers, visa control numbers, complete dates of birth, addresses, DS-160 barcodes, financial account details, minor children’s personal information or any identifying data not expressly approved by the client.
